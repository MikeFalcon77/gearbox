### Critical

- **A `#[serde(with)]` field loses its secret classification, so a `SecretString` credential projects as non-secret.** — `crates/gearbox-project/src/config.rs:508` (`RUST-SEC-001`)
  - **Why:** `attrs.with` forces `secret: false` even when the field's type says it is a credential. `#[serde(with = "...")] token: SecretString` projects as a non-secret field, and `ConfigField.secret` is the only gate `gearbox-engine` has for keeping a credential out of the generated ConfigMap (see `generate/helm.rs` and `secrets.rs`). The wire shape being unreadable is a reason to report `Complex`, not a reason to forget the field is a secret.
  - **Fix:** Compute `secret` from `classify(optional.unwrap_or(&field.ty), files)` regardless of `with`/`flatten`, and only override the `ConfigFieldType` to `Complex`.
